MY REVIEW

Overrated Sci-Fi Mess

Aetherium tries so hard to be the next Interstellar, but it ends up feeling more like a two-hour screensaver with a bad soundtrack. While the visual effects and CGI budget clearly hit the screen, the plot is an utterly nonsensical slog filled with endless, dreary exposition about quantum mechanics that the writers clearly didn't understand themselves. The lead actor delivers every single line in a hushed, raspy whisper—presumably to convey deep existential drama, though it mostly just makes you wish you had subtitles on. Save your money, skip the theater, and rewatch a classic instead.

Justin Ken Ramoso
